### Data/paper information
This paper replicates a study published in 2026 titled, "Understanding landowner preferences for traditional and nature-based solutions incentive programs in North Carolina, USA" written by Courtney B Deviney, Rajan Parajuli, Frederick Cubbage, Erin Sills, 
and Robert Bardon. The paper can be found here - https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0347042#sec009. A PDF of the data can be found in the 'Supporting Information' section of the link, although additional outreach might need 
to be done to get the data. We gained access to the data in October of 2026.
